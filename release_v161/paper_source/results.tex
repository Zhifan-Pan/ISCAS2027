\input{language_scope.tex}
True empty text with $T=0$ and no PLL targets has $f(\emptyset)=(0,0,0,0)$ and $\beta_F^\top x_i(\emptyset)=-\beta_F^\top f(b_i)$. Its margin $-\beta_F^\top f(y)$ is positive under signs $(+,+,-,-)$ when $a,p\le0$, $t>0$ and $r\ge0$, even at $r=0$. Centering preserves this preference, not a transcript probability. An unfitted coefficient on $e(y)=\mathbf1\{y=\emptyset\}$ could shift acceptance; a fixed nonempty guard raises CV27 S+R WER to 31.88\%. Reverting 18 accepted empty HF instances in 11 recordings to B removes 8.48\% of gain, leaving 2.10\,pp.

Acceptance removes 1,995 insertions and 118 substitutions but adds 120 deletions, consistent with hallucination suppression~\cite{baranski2025nonspeech}. Without frame-level silence labels, this establishes neither pure silence nor safety on short speech.
