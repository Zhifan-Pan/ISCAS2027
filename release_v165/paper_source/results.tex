\subsection{Acceptance changes recovery opportunities}
Direct lowers CV27 WER by bypassing G4 on triggers despite retaining only 67.13\% of its own positive edit gain, against Shared's 99.98\%, using full-language denominators. Relative to unconditional final HF, it forgoes 1.24\,pp of beneficial changes but avoids 3.58\,pp of harmful changes, so positive gain alone does not determine accuracy.

Shared beats Direct by 0.53\,pp with greedy recovery and 0.43\,pp with one $T=0.2$ draw. HF reverses the ranking by 0.22\,pp, with a 95\% interval from 0.11 to 0.37\,pp. This advantage comes from one checkpoint, with slight regressions in the other two. DEV favored HF reselection at 30.08\% versus 30.09\%, motivating fixed acceptance.

Under signs $(+,+,-,-)$, the margin $-\beta_F^\top f(y)>0$ favors emptiness when $a,p\le0$, $t>0$ and $r\ge0$, even at $r=0$. This ranks candidates rather than assigning transcript probabilities. Reverting all 18 empty acceptances in 11 CV27 recordings to B retains 91.52\% of gain, or 2.10\,pp at 31.88\% WER. The counts suggest hallucination suppression~\cite{baranski2025nonspeech}, but acoustic attribution requires silence labels (Table~\ref{tab:language}). \mbox{H4+$e$ adds} $e(y)=\mathbf1\{y=\emptyset\}$ on H4's DEV folds and grid. With one or two DEV empties per checkpoint, it matches H4's rounded 31.52\% WER and retains all 18 acceptances.
