# Evidence scope

- The original confirmation supports the frozen acquisition policies. Direct's
  reconstruction and paired interval are retrospective. H4/HWR4 full candidate
  features are unavailable on that panel; their comparisons use exposed TEST.
- An interval crossing zero does not prove equivalence or future zero risk.
  Speaker overlap across Common Voice splits limits the sampling frame.
- Study A covers four paths over three checkpoints; study B covers seven paths
  at one checkpoint. Both use the existing stratified sample and three paired
  rounds. The latter is not a timing census of all recordings.
- Unsampled later triggers account for 0.05% of FLEURS checkpoint-recording
  instances and 0.12% of CV27. Those count shares do not bound a latency ratio's
  error. Identical candidate banks need not have exactly equal measured time.
- Strategy mean differences and an unweighted 25-recording stage profile are
  different estimands. Singleton scoring skips do not justify equating them.
- Complete refit-policy and trigger-selective PLL costs remain unmeasured.
  Allocated memory includes both resident models.
- The cached nonempty guard retains 91.52% of CV27 gain and excludes empty
  outputs. It does not eliminate deletions within nonempty hypotheses, identify
  the acoustic cause, or establish unseen short-speech safety.
- H4+e retains all 18 accepted empty instances; matching H4 after rounding is
  not exact count or hypothesis equality. Live window-local RNG may differ from
  the fixed-candidate diagnostic. Language and checkpoint effects remain coupled.
