Experiments were completed before the v160 presentation/publication update.
Identifiers 101, 202 and 303 identify the three original random seeds.

- Original G4 policies retain their selector, CR trigger and HF source.
- H4, HR4 and H3 retain nine final fits, five component folds and 49 penalties.
- HF9/HF12 retain their labels, thresholds and all nine quantiles.
- HWR4 and MBR retain their original DEV procedures.
- Cost A measures four policies at three checkpoints. Cost B measures seven
  policies at one checkpoint. These scopes must not be pooled.

WER aggregates edits and reference lengths within language before averaging
languages and checkpoints. Calls pool checkpoint-recording instances. Original
paired component bootstrap draws synchronize checkpoints; registered contrasts
and intervals remain in `acceptors/expected/`.

The 7,500-recording confirmation supports its original frozen policies only.
Later gates, acceptors, Direct diagnostics and window controls are retrospective.
Release verification and CLI help are software checks, not new experiments.
