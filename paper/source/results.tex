\subsection{Acquisition with fixed acceptance}
On the locked confirmation panel, S+R gains 2.26\,pp over B, with seed gains of 0.20 to 4.54\,pp (Table~\ref{tab:paths}). Matching N+R's error counts in every seed meets the prespecified 0.10\,pp margin but does not guarantee zero loss under distribution shift. Of 61 triggers, 49 improve edits and two worsen them.

Retrospective evaluation yields 32.71\% WER for Direct versus S+R's locked 32.75\%. Both beat B and Native HF's retrospective 34.87\% by over 2.1\,pp. Direct's unrounded difference from S+R is $-0.05$\,pp with a paired speaker-bootstrap 95\% interval from $-0.19$ to $+0.04$\,pp.

On TEST, S+R retains all ten beneficial FLEURS instances at 0.09\% calls and 370 of 373 CV27 instances at 0.30\%, missing five edits. Language-reference normalization gives 99.98\% macro-weighted positive edit gain retention. N+R selects HF on one FLEURS and 13 CV27 nontriggers where S+R keeps B, with nearly identical WER. Reference-assisted oracles minimize edits over concatenated original, expanded or routed banks. Routing changes this bound by under 0.01~pp against S+R's 4.00~pp CV27 gap. In retrospective TEST replay, the word gate $r_{4,3}(b_i) > 0$ lowers FLEURS gain retention from 100\% to 93.34\% (0.06\% calls; 99.21\% at 0.18\% on CV27), whereas token-ID compression $> 1.35$ more than doubles FLEURS calls to 0.20\% (0.31\% on CV27) for 100\%/99.99\% retention, supporting the text gate as a parsimonious operating point on these banks.

\subsection{Interaction with the fallback source}
S+R beats Direct on unscreened fallbacks by 0.53~pp with greedy recovery and 0.43~pp with one draw at temperature 0.2. HF screens retries by compression, log-probability and no-speech criteria. Direct then leads G4 by 0.22~pp, with a 95\% interval from 0.11 to 0.37~pp, concentrated in checkpoint one ($-0.68$~pp versus $+0.00$ and $+0.02$~pp). There, greedy-fitted G4 retains repetitive beams whose decoder/PLL advantage outweighs length and repetition penalties, rejecting better HF candidates. DEV favored HF reselection at 30.08\% versus 30.09\% and guided the fixed acquisition control.

Direct retains 67.13\% of positive gain from unconditional final HF, compared with S+R's 99.98\% from N+R. Relative to its own control, Direct forgoes 1.24~pp of benefit but avoids 3.58~pp of harm.

On HF banks, H4 improves over G4 but trails Direct (31.52\% versus 31.46\% CV27 WER). HWR4 yields the lowest complete CV27 WER of 31.44\% (Table~\ref{tab:hfacceptors}, where S denotes each acceptor's selective policy). Edit-gap weighting reverses the mean contrast to $-0.08$~pp through a 0.25~pp gain in checkpoint two that replaces 11 repetitive beams with lower-edit HF candidates. Intervals provide 95\% Bonferroni coverage per family, with 10,000 synchronized draws for the later family. Using G4's language/bank-weighted RMS instead of pooled RMS for HF refits changes CV27 WER by $+0.044$, $-0.057$ and $+0.003$\,pp for H4, HR4 and HWR4, respectively, without changing their ordering.

FLEURS gains round to 0.37\,pp for fitted acceptors because S equals its own B off-trigger. H3's B has two fewer errors on one of ten triggers, but S errors match and the average difference is below 0.006\,pp. Nontriggers account for 97.23\% of the refitted H3--H4 WER gap in routine selection, without isolating PLL's causal contribution or time share.

\subsection{Cost, heterogeneity and supporting diagnostics}
Within reranked pipelines, S+R preserves N+R's gain at lower cost (Table~\ref{tab:cost}). Study B's rescoring increments above raw top-1 are 141.4\,ms and 125.2\,ms on FLEURS and CV27. Selective acquisition saves 87.7\% and 56.6\% of fallback overhead. The unweighted 25-recording CV27 profile averages 85.9\,ms teacher scoring and 149.3\,ms PLL, including two skipped singletons. More singletons skip both stages in the population, explaining the smaller weighted increments. Native HF skips rescoring, trading faster decoding for 1.46\,pp higher FLEURS WER and over 2.1\,pp on CV27 TEST.

H3Direct uses H3 off-trigger and HF on triggers, omitting PLL. On CV27 it saves 6.29\% over Direct but trails Native HF by 55.2\,ms (95\% interval 52.3 to 58.2\,ms); the FLEURS interval spans zero. H4 and HWR4 only replace G4's weights; selective PLL remains future work. In Study B, Direct skips scoring on the CV27 trigger responsible for B's peak CUDA memory. On FLEURS, Direct and H3Direct match S+R's peak CUDA allocation when both windows' states are retained during recovery.
