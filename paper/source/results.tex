\subsection{Acquisition with fixed acceptance}
On the locked confirmation panel, S+R gains 2.26\,pp over B, with seed gains of 0.20 to 4.54\,pp (Table~\ref{tab:paths}). Matching N+R's error counts in every seed meets the prespecified 0.10\,pp margin but does not guarantee zero loss under distribution shift. Of 61 triggers, 49 improve edits and two worsen them.

Retrospective evaluation yields 32.71\% WER for Direct versus S+R's locked 32.75\%. Both beat B and Native HF's retrospective 34.87\% by over 2.1\,pp. Direct's unrounded difference from S+R is $-0.05$\,pp with a paired speaker-bootstrap 95\% interval from $-0.19$ to $+0.04$\,pp.

On TEST, S+R retains all ten beneficial FLEURS instances at 0.09\% calls and 370 of 373 CV27 instances at 0.30\%, with five edits missed. Retention is 99.98\% of available positive edit gain after language-reference normalization and averaging over languages and seeds. Normalized outputs differ on one FLEURS and 13 CV27 nontriggers despite nearly identical WER. On these nontriggers, S+R keeps B, but N+R requests $g_i$ and reselects from the expanded bank. Reference-assisted oracles minimize recording edits over concatenated original, expanded or CR-routed banks. Routing changes this bound by under 0.01\,pp versus CV27 S+R's 4.00\,pp gap, so acceptance offers greater headroom.

\subsection{Interaction with the fallback source}
S+R can reject unscreened fallbacks and beats Direct by 0.53\,pp with greedy recovery and 0.43\,pp with one draw at temperature 0.2. HF screens retries by compression, log-probability and no-speech criteria. The greedy-fitted G4 can instead favor an original beam over a screened recovery. Direct then leads by 0.22\,pp, with a 95\% interval from 0.11 to 0.37\,pp. The gain comes from one checkpoint ($-0.68$\,pp versus $+0.00$ and $+0.02$\,pp). DEV favored HF reselection at 30.08\% versus 30.09\% and guided the fixed acquisition control.

Direct retains 67.13\% of positive gain from unconditional final HF, compared with S+R's 99.98\% from N+R. Relative to its own control, Direct forgoes 1.24\,pp of benefit but avoids 3.58\,pp of harm.

\subsection{Cost, heterogeneity and supporting diagnostics}
S+R preserves N+R's accuracy gain at lower overhead for systems using B (Table~\ref{tab:cost}). Study B measures 141.4\,ms and 125.2\,ms for rescoring above raw top-1 on FLEURS and CV27. S+R saves 87.7\% and 56.6\% of N+R's additional recovery cost. An unweighted 25-recording CV27 profile averages teacher scoring at 85.9\,ms and PLL at 149.3\,ms, including two singletons that skip both. These conditional costs motivate selective PLL, but the larger population singleton share prevents a direct stage decomposition. Native HF skips N-best rescoring and is faster at a WER cost of 1.46\,pp on FLEURS and over 2.1\,pp on CV27 TEST.

H3Direct uses H3 off-trigger and final HF on triggers, and omits PLL like raw top-1. On CV27 it saves 6.29\% over Direct but trails Native HF by 55.2\,ms, with a 95\% interval of 52.3 to 58.2\,ms; the FLEURS interval spans zero.
H4 and HWR4 only replace G4's weights; selective PLL remains future work. In Study B, Direct bypasses scoring on the CV27 trigger responsible for B's peak CUDA memory. On FLEURS, Direct and H3Direct match S+R's peak CUDA allocation in two-window recovery with both windows' state retained.
