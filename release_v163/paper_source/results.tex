\input{language_scope.tex}
Original TEST beam banks contain no empty transcripts; empty candidates enter through HF fallback. True empty text gives $f(\emptyset)=(0,0,0,0)$ and $\beta_F^\top x_i(\emptyset)=-\beta_F^\top f(b_i)$. Its margin is $-\beta_F^\top f(y)>0$ under signs $(+,+,-,-)$ when $a,p\le0$, $t>0$ and $r\ge0$, even at $r=0$. Centering preserves this preference, not a transcript probability.

Forbidding 18 empty HF acceptances in 11 CV27 recordings reverts those outputs to B: 91.52\% of the gain, or 2.10\,pp, survives at 31.88\% WER. Acceptance trades insertions for deletions (Table~\ref{tab:language}), consistent with hallucination suppression~\cite{baranski2025nonspeech} but without silence labels. The guard excludes empty outputs, not all word deletions.
